Cybersecurity

We live in an era where practically all of our activities are connected to the internet: studying, working, shopping, communicating, and even managing our healthcare. While this reliance on technology brings countless benefits, it also exposes individuals and organizations to increasingly greater risks. It is within this landscape that cybersecurity becomes fundamental, ensuring that data and systems remain protected against cyberattacks.
